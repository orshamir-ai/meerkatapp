About

A small game about protecting something.

Meerkat Universe is a mobile game for young players. You are the guardian of a treehouse, and hungry visitors keep climbing up for your fruit.

One swipe moves you between branches. That is the whole control — easy to learn at five, and steadily harder to keep up with as the levels go on.

What is in it

Simple to pick up. Plenty to master.

Three branches

Swipe to move the guardian between lanes. Pinecones fly on their own — your job is being in the right place.

Four kinds of visitor

Monkeys, quick squirrels, tough raccoons — and a fox you will not see coming until it breaks cover.

Special attacks

Earn beetles, then spend them on a Freeze, a Shield or a Barrage for the rounds that get away from you.

Friends and scores

Add a friend with a code and see how far along everyone is on the leaderboard.
