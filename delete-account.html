Delete Account

How to close a Meerkat Universe account.

In the app

Account deletion is available inside the app itself:

  • Open Settings from the home screen.
  • Go to the Account section.
  • Choose Delete Account and confirm.

The app will ask for confirmation before anything is deleted. Deleting an account is not reversible, and progress saved to that account cannot be recovered afterwards.

Deleting the account is not the same as deleting the app. Removing the app from a device leaves the account and its saved progress in place; deleting the account removes the saved progress from the account.

If you cannot reach the in-app option

Deletion needs a working connection, because it removes data held outside the device. If the option does not complete, try again on a working connection.

There is no published request route yet, and no published timescale.

A parent or player who cannot use the in-app option currently has no documented way to ask for deletion, because no contact address has been published. That is a gap, not a policy.

This page also makes no claim about how long deletion takes, exactly which records are removed, or what is retained and for how long. Those have not been documented, and stating them here without that would be a guarantee nobody has made.
